# Synthetic evidence for a ledger demonstration

These records illustrate the shape of evidence and decisions. They do not claim
that an exporter was implemented, a compatibility test passed, or a user benefited.

The imaginary comparison favours a simple format. The imaginary follow-up records
that consumer compatibility remains unmeasured. Its result is submitted, awaiting
an applicable decision. The first task's fictional acceptance demonstrates the
selector's dependency rule. Actual ledger behavior is tested by test_ledger.py.
