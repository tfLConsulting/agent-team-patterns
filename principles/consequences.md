# Measure what a result changes

**Principle:** assess the significance of a finding separately from the safety of applying a change. A harmless edit can correct a premise behind an entire plan.

Here, “finding significance” means a possible change to beliefs or decisions. This is narrower than the ordinary meaning of consequence, which also includes harm, cost, exposure, timing, reversibility, and affected people. Choose the dimensions the domain needs; belief change is not a universal severity model.

Ask: what did we believe before; what evidence arrived; what can we now reasonably believe; whose decision changes; and when must they know? For example, a fictional export investigation may find that nobody uses the format the roadmap prioritised. The code change can be tiny while the planning consequence is large.

Keep three views distinct:

- **Delivery:** what artifact exists and where it is integrated or released.
- **Learning:** what assumption was supported, weakened, or falsified.
- **Outcome:** what changed for a beneficiary after use, over a stated period.

Task counts and test results address delivery and controls. They do not establish outcome. For a planned improvement, name a baseline, measure, observation window, source, confounders, and what result would change the plan. If benefit cannot yet be measured, record that gap and a feasible observation trigger.

Consequence categories can help routing: local detail, changed assumption, changed direction. Give each rating a reason; numbers are not measurements simply because they are sortable. Have another reader assess important findings from original evidence when the cost of a missed assumption warrants it. Do not require a second agent for every spelling correction.

Measurements need population, date, definition, and uncertainty. Missing data is not zero. A lower bound can establish that exposure exceeds a threshold; falling below the threshold does not establish safety. Recheck an instrument after changing its rules, data source, or target population. Read [measurement skepticism](../methodology/measurement-skepticism.md) with its historical qualifications.

**Failure modes:** report a discovery only inside a diff; reward task throughput; claim benefit before observation; reuse a precision estimate from an old corpus; let a significance score silently become permission to act. Route the finding even when the implementation can proceed under existing policy. Whether significance also gates implementation is a local authority/risk policy: separating the questions does not require independent decision lanes in every project.
