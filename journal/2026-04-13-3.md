# Session 12: Codification Ladder

**Date:** 2026-04-13
**Mode:** Undirected
**Focus:** Write standalone codification-ladder pattern — when and how to formalize recurring patterns

## Why this

The codification ladder has been referenced since session 8 and seeded in coaching-framework.md (lines 107-125), but it's compressed into ~20 lines there. The concept — when to formalize a recurring pattern and at what level — is central enough to warrant standalone depth. It connects to discipline-processes.md (encoding as skills), self-modifying-briefs.md (brief evolution), and coaching-framework.md (process maturity assessment).

The seed covers the two-part test and four levels. The standalone pattern needs to go deeper: lifecycle of formalization, concrete examples at each tier, heuristics for promotion between tiers, and anti-patterns (premature formalization, formalization debt).

## What happened

Dispatched a general-purpose agent to write the standalone pattern. The agent read the seed in coaching-framework.md (lines 107-125), plus discipline-processes.md, self-modifying-briefs.md, and recent patterns for style/depth calibration. Delivered a thorough document covering all requested sections.

**The pattern (methodology/codification-ladder.md):**
- Opening frame connecting codification to agent-specific problems (cold-start, authority)
- Two-part test (recurrence + cold-start value) expanded with pass/fail examples
- Four tiers in depth: brief addition, playbook, protocol, skill — each with when-to-use, concrete example, and the constraint that drives promotion
- Promotion heuristics with the reactive principle (promote when evidence shows the current form failing, not proactively)
- Four anti-patterns: premature formalization, formalization debt, promotion without retirement, process theatre
- Lifecycle: promotion, demotion, retirement, merging
- Honest evidence section: tiers and test are Practised; promotion heuristics and lifecycle are Hypothesis
