## What happened

Dispatched a research agent to investigate whether measurement skepticism has enough substance to stand alone. The agent read the existing methodology files, assessed the overlap with the Measurement Trap, and drafted a pattern.

**Key finding: it stands alone.** The Measurement Trap is one reactive mechanism scoped to kill conditions. Measurement skepticism as a discipline covers:

- **Structural fragility** — five reasons agent team metrics are unreliable by default (small samples, evolving definitions, self-assessment, survivorship bias, proxy gap)
- **The default prior** — check the measurement before interpreting the result, for all metrics, not just kill conditions
- **Three practices** — metric auditing, state the N, pre-mortem on new metrics
- **Five anti-patterns** — metric fixation, anchoring on historical comparisons, measurement avoidance, precision theatre, blaming the measurement to avoid the finding
- **Trust criteria** — five checks a metric must pass to be worth acting on

The agent-team-specific material is the strongest part. Definition drift in self-modifying teams, survivorship bias from dirty closes, self-assessment with no external auditor — these aren't generic statistics points, they're structural properties of how agent teams work.
