# Session 5: Reconciling the Bootstrapping Path

**Date:** 2026-04-03
**Mode:** Undirected
**Focus:** Editorial roadmap item #2 — reconcile bootstrapping-protocol.md with agent-team-guide.md Section 4

## Why this

The methodology review (session 2) identified that bootstrapping steps are split across two files: `bootstrapping-protocol.md` covers the philosophy and reasoning, while `agent-team-guide.md` Section 4 provides the actionable walkthrough. A reader trying to bootstrap a team hits redundancy and has to reconcile the two sources themselves. This is the second-highest priority editorial improvement.

## What happened

Dispatched a sub-agent for detailed overlap analysis. The findings were decisive: of bootstrapping-protocol.md's ~85 lines, roughly 55-60 were already covered (usually better) in the guide. Only ~25-30 lines of genuinely unique content existed:

- "The Hard Question" framing — why creating a team is harder than creating agents
- "Wiring in the Learning Loop" — experiment log, micro-observation prompt, first-10-sessions-as-exploration
- "Cargo culting" and "wrong composition" as named failure modes
- "Bootstrap paradox" as a concept

Decided on **Option B: merge unique content into the guide, retire bootstrapping-protocol.md.** Same pattern as session 4's team-coordination.md retirement. The reasoning: extensive overlap, the guide's treatments are stronger in every shared area, and a single authoritative source is better for a public patterns library.

## Edits made

Six content moves into `agent-team-guide.md`:
1. "Hard Question" framing added to Section 4 intro (motivates the bootstrapping sequence)
2. "Cargo Culting Patterns" added as Section 3.7
3. "Wrong composition" paragraph appended to Section 3.6
4. "Wire in the Learning Loop" added as Section 4.7 (former 4.7 renumbered to 4.8)
5. Bootstrap paradox merged into "Replace operator judgment" in Section 12
6. "Platform evolution" note appended to LLM transfer paragraph in Section 12

Section 10 checklist updated for new numbering. Cross-references updated in `README.md` and `methodology/README.md`. `bootstrapping-protocol.md` deleted.

Net: ~40-50 lines added to the guide, one file retired, all unique content preserved in better locations, all redundancy eliminated.

## Practice observation

This is the second file retirement following the same pattern (team-coordination.md in session 4, bootstrapping-protocol.md in session 5). The sub-agent approach worked well — the overlap analysis required reading both files in full detail, which would have cluttered the team lead's context. Having the analysis written to scratchpad and then dispatching a separate editing agent kept the routing context clean. Two-agent dispatch (analyze, then execute) is proving to be the right granularity for editorial work.
