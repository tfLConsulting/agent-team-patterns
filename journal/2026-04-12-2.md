## Practice Observation

The "upgrade the tool, then use it" sequence paid off. Writing the coaching-framework pattern first meant the coach brief upgrade was grounded in a well-structured document rather than raw scratchpad notes. And the Review 2 immediately exercised the new framework, giving us a concrete test of whether it works at our scale. The coach's self-reflection — that backward validation was more valuable than dimension scoring at this team size — is exactly the kind of signal the self-reflection mechanism is designed to produce. It suggests that for small teams, the framework's value is less in the dimension grid and more in the discipline of tracking recommendations and watching for specific signals.
